---
description: Review the current change against the architecture principles
---

Review the working tree — or the branch against its base, if the working tree is clean — using the
`arch-reviewer` subagent.

Give the subagent the diff scope and let it read what it needs. Relay its findings, ordered by cost,
and add nothing of your own unless it missed something you can name specifically.

If it reports no findings, say so plainly rather than padding the report.
